Privacy Policy

How BlueCatch handles information submitted through this website.

Information you provide

When you submit an order enquiry, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, order preferences and other information needed to respond.

How information is used

Information is used to answer enquiries, discuss availability, prepare order details and improve the usefulness of the market experience.

Cookies and preferences

This template stores light and dark mode preference and direction preference in the browser using local storage. These settings do not contain order information.

Sharing

Information should only be shared with people or service providers who need it to support the requested enquiry or website operation.

Retention

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and applicable operational requirements.

Your choices

You may ask what information is associated with an enquiry and request correction of inaccurate contact details, subject to applicable requirements.
