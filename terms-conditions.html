Terms & Conditions

Clear expectations for using the BlueCatch website and submitting seafood enquiries.

1. Website information

Product descriptions, market notes, recipe guidance and availability information are provided to help customers make informed enquiries. Actual catch, size, cut and availability can change with market conditions.

2. Order enquiries

An enquiry is a request for information or availability and is not automatically a confirmed order. A member of the market desk should confirm the final details before preparation or dispatch.

3. Availability and substitutions

When a requested item is unavailable, BlueCatch may suggest a comparable alternative based on texture, cooking method or intended use. Customers can accept or decline an alternative before confirmation.

4. Customer details

Customers should provide accurate contact information and useful order details. We use submitted information to respond to enquiries and coordinate the requested service.

5. Website use

Users agree to use the website lawfully and not to interfere with its operation, content or security.

6. Changes

These terms may be updated as the website, services or market process changes. The current published version applies to website use.
