Before you enquire

Give us the details that make the answer useful

The more context you share, the easier it is for the market desk to suggest the right fish, quantity, preparation and timing.

Tell us the seafood

Name the fish, shellfish or recipe you have in mind. If you are unsure, describe the meal and we can help narrow the options.

Tell us the quantity

Share the number of diners, expected portions or approximate weight so we can make the enquiry more practical.

Tell us the timing

Include your preferred date, collection or delivery window and any occasion that makes the timing important.

Order journey

What happens after you send the form?

01 · We read the request. Your fish, quantity, preparation and timing details are reviewed together.

02 · We check the market. Availability is matched against the current catch and suitable alternatives.

03 · We confirm the plan. You receive the practical next step for preparation, collection or delivery.

04 · We prepare the handoff. The final order details are kept clear so there is less uncertainty at collection or delivery.

Common questions

Quick answers before you order

Every answer is collapsed by default so you can scan the topics first.

Can I ask for a specific preparation?

Yes. Include whole, cleaned, portioned or filleted preferences in the enquiry. Availability depends on the day’s supply and fish type.

Can I track my order?

After confirmation, use the order details supplied by the market desk for the relevant collection or delivery update.

What if my preferred fish is unavailable?

We can suggest a similar option based on texture, cooking method and budget, so you have a useful alternative rather than a dead end.

Put the whole meal in the message.

Fish type, guest count, recipe idea and timing are enough for our team to start a useful conversation.
