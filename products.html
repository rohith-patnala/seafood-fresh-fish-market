Cooking match

Pick the seafood that fits the meal

Match texture and format to the cooking method you already have in mind.

For grilling

Choose firm fish or generous prawns that can hold their shape over high heat. Citrus, herbs and simple spice blends work beautifully.

For curries

Look for flavorful fish with enough structure to remain tender through simmering. Mackerel and firm white fish are versatile starting points.

For quick meals

Fillets and cleaned prawns shorten prep time and pair easily with rice, vegetables, noodles, salads or flatbreads.
