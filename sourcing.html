Our sourcing approach

Know where your seafood story begins.

We make sourcing conversations clearer by sharing practical information about the catch, market availability, handling, and preparation choices before you enquire.

Explore seafood Ask about sourcing
Clarity first

Useful details, not vague promises.

Our sourcing approach is built around practical questions customers can act on: what species is available, how it is usually prepared, which cut suits the meal, and what the market can confirm for the day.

Availability changes with the catch and market conditions, so we focus on honest updates rather than blanket claims.

What we look at

Four useful parts of a sourcing conversation

Each order starts with details that help match the seafood to your needs.

Species and type

Identify the fish or shellfish and discuss similar alternatives when a preferred option is unavailable.

Handling needs

Consider cold handling, preparation preferences and the practical timing between purchase and cooking.

Quantity and cut

Share weight, guest count and whether you prefer whole, cleaned, portioned or filleted seafood.

Cooking purpose

Choose with your recipe in mind, from quick pan-searing to grilling, steaming and coastal curries.

Market availability

Today’s supply guides the final choice.

Fish type, size and volume can vary from day to day. When you enquire, we check what is available and explain suitable alternatives where possible.

Helpful tip: If your recipe is flexible, include your cooking method and budget range so the market desk can suggest options that fit.

Responsible communication

Clear about what we can confirm.

We avoid making unsupported claims about every item’s origin or availability. Instead, we encourage customers to ask about the specific seafood they want, and our market desk will share the details available for that selection.

This helps keep expectations practical and gives you a better basis for choosing your seafood.

Sourcing FAQs

Questions worth asking before you order

Can I ask where a particular fish comes from?

Yes. Ask about the specific item in your enquiry and the market desk can share the sourcing information available for that selection.

Is every fish available every day?

No. Availability can change with market supply. We will confirm the current options and suggest alternatives where possible.

Can I request a particular cut?

Include your preference for whole, cleaned, portioned or filleted seafood. Preparation options depend on the species and current service availability.
